Philosophy

Teaching Philosophy

Dr. Ghada believes every student is capable of understanding science when it is taught clearly, patiently, and in a relatable context.

1. Understanding Over Memorisation

Students are encouraged to understand concepts deeply rather than memorise facts, fostering long-term retention and genuine scientific thinking.

2. Confidence Building

A supportive environment where students feel safe to ask questions and make mistakes as part of the learning process.

3. Practical Application

Connecting scientific theory to real-world examples so students understand the relevance and excitement of science in everyday life.
