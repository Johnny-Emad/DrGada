Methodology

Teaching Approach

Dr. Ghada's methodology is designed to serve students with varying levels of prior knowledge and learning styles.

Step-by-Step Explanation

Each concept is introduced gradually, building from basic principles to complex applications so no student is overwhelmed.

Visual and Diagrammatic Learning

Diagrams and visual representations are used extensively to help students visualise abstract scientific concepts.

Worked Examples & Practice Problems

Extensive use of worked examples followed by guided practice ensures students can apply knowledge independently.

Regular Revision and Testing

Periodic quizzes and revision sessions to consolidate learning and identify areas needing further attention.

Exam Technique Coaching

Specific guidance on approaching exam questions, managing time, and maximising marks in official examinations.

Class Types

Lesson Categories

Classes at Center Future Academy are organised into the following categories.

Regular Weekly Lessons

Scheduled weekly classes following the school curriculum at each educational level. Primary classes for enrolled students.

Intensive Revision Sessions

Focused revision programmes ahead of final examinations. Open to all students including those not in regular classes.

Catch-Up / Remedial Classes

Sessions for students who have fallen behind or joined mid-year, bringing them up to speed with their peers.

Private One-to-One Lessons

Individual sessions for students who need personalised attention and a customised learning pace.
